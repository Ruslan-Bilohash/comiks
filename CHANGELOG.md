# Changelog

All notable changes to Komiks·Lab. The same log is shown inside the app under **✨ What’s new**.

## 2026-09-26 🎓

- 📅 “Today” — a ten-minute session: words due for review, the next step of your plan and work on your mistakes. Three things and the day is done.
- 📬 Email is fixed: confirmation and password-reset letters arrive again.
- 🃏 Flashcards now know all 972 topic words, not just the comic glossary — what you learn by topic comes back for review exactly when you start forgetting it.
- ✍️ Writing in Norwegian with feedback: write a few sentences and see the corrected text, every mistake explained in your language, and a model answer.
- 🎧 Listening to whole sentences: the line is spoken, the text stays hidden — you have to hear it and pick the right sentence.
- 🏁 A Norskprøven mock test: listening, reading and writing with an approximate level.
- 🎯 A level test: twelve questions, and your plan starts where it actually should.
- 🎙️ The pronunciation button is now on word cards too: say the word out loud and get a score.

## 2026-09-25 📲

- 📲 A separate “App” page with steps for Windows, Android and iPhone — no App Store, no Google Play, it installs straight from the browser.
- 📝 The word list has grown to 972 words in 47 topics: numbers, directions, question words, children and kindergarten, car and traffic, post, winter, cooking, digital services, safety, school and parents, NAV and rights.
- 🖼️ Sixty more drawn icons instead of look-alike emoji: mother, father, brother, sister, son and daughter finally look different, and the new words have pictures of their own.
- 🖼️ A new icon set: a crisp shortcut on the desktop and in the Start menu, a round icon on Android, a proper splash screen on iPhone, and a right-click menu with plan, words, call and tests.
- 🎬 The verb room: a separate room with interactive comics — in the games list, in the tests and in the footer.
- ⬆️ The header slides away as you scroll down and comes back at the slightest scroll up — more room for the text on a phone.
- 🔊 Spoken praise (“Veldig bra!”, “Prøv igjen!”) now sounds in every test, not just a few.

## 2026-09-24 📚

- 📚 Thirty new B1 and B2 comics: NAV, renting a flat, the dentist, the Norwegian exam, taxes, mortgages, insurance, parental leave, tenant rights and more — 65 stories in total.
- 🔒 Strict mode for parents: the learner follows the plan step by step, everything else stays locked, and only the parent password switches it off.
- 📱 A ready base for the App Store and Google Play apps — the same site inside.
- 📝 The dictionary has grown to 2010 Norwegian words with translations.
- 🖼️ New drawn icons instead of unclear emoji — in tests, word lists and flashcards.
- 📖 Picture questions now show which comic they come from: one tap takes you to that panel.
- 📲 An “Install the app” button: a shortcut on your phone or in the Start menu, the site runs like a normal app.
- 🎁 The “30 free hours” promise is gone — the “first 100” offer and the subscription replace it.
- 💌 The feedback form opens as a proper window again, not below the footer.

## 2026-09-23 📞

- 📞 A call in Norwegian: a fictional character rings you — real ringing, a phone screen and a short talk. The voice is neural, like in the comics.
- 📊 “My learning analysis”: your most frequent mistakes, the weakest tests and a repeat test built from them.
- 📝 730 words to learn instead of 394: work, housing, money, public services, travel, technology, character, culture.
- 📚 Two new comics: “At the pharmacy” (A2) and “The appraisal talk” (B2).
- 🤖 Ten more virtual players — the ranking feels alive.
- 💌 The “Feedback” button moved to the footer and the share block so it covers nothing.

## 2026-09-22 🏅

- 🏅 The first 100 members get Premium free forever — and may remove bad posts from walls.
- 🎓 Teachers get Premium for free: send your proof from the “For teachers” page.
- 💌 A “Feedback” button on every page: a bug, an idea or a learning topic — with a photo or a file.
- 📚 Three new comics: synes/tror and the possessive words (min/mi/mitt/mine, sin/hans/hennes, vår, deres).
- 🎚️ Two new test difficulties: 🔥 hard and 🏆 expert — with a timer on every question.
- 🔎 Comics in Google search: every comic page now has drawings, a cover and data for image search.
